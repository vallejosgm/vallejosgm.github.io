Overview

CeciStyle Operations Platform is an evolving production system developed around the operational needs of an alterations and design business. The application centralizes workflows that would otherwise be distributed across appointments, customer records, inventory, payments, garment intake, production planning, and physical garment tracking.

My work focuses on translating real business processes into maintainable software: understanding how staff actually work, identifying operational risks and repetitive tasks, modeling the required data, and building connected workflows around those requirements.

The Business Problem

An alterations business has to coordinate much more than appointments. Staff need to maintain customer identity, garments, requested work, payments, deadlines, production capacity, media permissions, physical locations, and pickup status. When these records live in disconnected tools or manual processes, operational context can be lost and avoidable scheduling, payment, or garment-tracking errors can occur.

The goal of the platform is to turn those separate activities into connected, traceable business workflows.

What I Built

Booking & Scheduling

Availability-driven public appointment booking with validation, controlled scheduling, and clear confirmation workflows.

Alteration Operations

Multi-garment alteration orders connecting customers, requested work, payment state, media permissions, production milestones, workload, and garment details.

Production Capacity Planning

Production workload is translated into visible capacity percentages so constrained dates can be identified before additional work is accepted or assigned.

Inventory & Barcode Tracking

Permanent inventory identifiers, barcode-assisted lookup, status, condition, location, images, and audit metadata support traceable physical inventory handling.

Payments & Square Integration

Square payment synchronization and payment-to-work matching connect external payment activity to internal operational records with transactional business rules.

Garment Tracking

Work tickets, location events, and chain-of-custody history keep physical garments connected to their operational records throughout production.

System Architecture

The system follows Laravel conventions while organizing larger business capabilities into domain-oriented modules. Controllers coordinate HTTP workflows, Form Requests validate input, service classes encapsulate business logic, Eloquent models represent the domain, and database transactions protect multi-record operations.

Engineering Highlights

Strategy Pattern for Customer Identity

Booking contact information can create ambiguous identity situations when email and phone data point to different customer records. Instead of silently merging records, the workflow uses a common strategy contract and explicit resolution paths for supported conflict scenarios.

Transactional Alteration Workflows

Alteration orders span multiple related operational records. Service-layer transaction boundaries keep creation and updates consistent when one business action affects several database entities.

Payment Matching Integrity

Payment matching incorporates transaction handling, row locking, ownership checks, reuse prevention, and amount validation to reduce the risk of connecting the wrong payment to work.

Operational Security Controls

The application uses authenticated sessions, role-based access controls, request validation, throttling, employee account controls, and no-store responses for administrative workflows.

Testing & Reliability

At the merge point of the integrated Alteration Operations release, the production repository reported 53 passing tests and 219 assertions. This is a historical verification point for that release rather than a code-coverage claim or a guarantee about later commits.

The public engineering repository intentionally contains selected, sanitized evidence rather than the complete production test suite or proprietary application source.
